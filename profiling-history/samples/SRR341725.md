sample-dependent** — only compare runs that used the *same* sample (and the same subsample). Raw FASTQ
lives in the gitignored `profiling/data/`; this doc is the committed record of what it is and where it

## Why this sample (and caveats for profiling)

- A **real human gut metagenome** → MEGAHIT's optimized target workload: high diversity, uneven coverage,
  a long tail of low-abundance taxa. Representative of the intended use, unlike a single-genome dataset.
- **Short, fixed 90 bp reads** (older GA II) → caps the usable k-mer ladder. MEGAHIT auto-reduces `k-max`;
  the baseline run's k-list stopped at 99. A modern 150 bp sample would allow larger k and shift the
  per-stage balance — **do not compare across samples with different read lengths** without noting it.
- GC ~43 % is typical for gut microbiome.
